Selected Alpine Campsite props in the Mountain001 dev scene

Original set: Alpine Campsite, version 1.0, authored with Blender 4.3.2.
Selected original assets: 04_ridge_picnic_table and 12_ranger_relief_map_station.
The individual full-detail GLBs are retained byte-for-byte under
models/alpine-campsite/glb/. Their SHA-256 digests are recorded in
models/alpine-campsite/selected-manifest.json. No other theme-pack assets,
collection presentation, original full collection scene, or reference artwork
are included in this integration.

The original package described its meshes and scene composition as original
procedural work authored for the user's request, with no third-party model,
texture, illustration, font or project code incorporated. It identified some
geometry helpers as part of an earlier self-authored theme pipeline. It did not
state a standalone license. Its provenance statement was explicitly not a
blanket license or ownership guarantee; applicable platform terms still apply.
This notice does not claim that the original package was CC0 or previously AGPL,
or supply a broader ownership/commercial-use warranty.

At the user's explicit request, these selected original props and their
reproducible source are integrated into the existing AGPL-3.0-only game. The
game's corresponding source and derivative integration are supplied under its
existing LICENSE; this preserves the original package's provenance and caveat.
The separately restricted character assets remain governed by MODEL-NOTICE.txt
and are not included in this prop integration.

Derivative changes are limited and reviewable:
- source/generate-selected.py retains the selected original geometry and helper
  expressions, excluding unrelated props and presentation/render operations
- scripts/convert-alpine-props.mjs reads only the two verified source GLBs and
  produces src/generated/alpine-props.json deterministically
- Original local positions, triangle indices/winding and UV0 are retained at
  unit scale; placement applies an explicit Y rotation and translation
- Original normals remain in the GLBs. The shared scene renderer deliberately
  recalculates normals from the same positions/topology, as for other scenery
- Original opaque PBR materials are remapped to the Mountain shared palette;
  material identities and original PBR values are retained in the derived data
  for review. Shared wood/stone texture, roughness, color and metalness can differ
- Complete projected footprints, exact source bounds, lowest source vertices
  and a separately labeled low support band are derived from all source geometry
- The Mountain scene seats both props on separately modeled stone pads with an
  explicit 3 cm bedding embed; the original local geometry is not reshaped
- The original map station contains separately authored relief solids with small
  spacing gaps, not a Boolean-unioned board. src/mountain-scenery.ts adds nine
  separate integration-only mounting boxes: two board-to-backing stand-offs,
  one lake boss, three snowcap bosses, one trail-pin backing and two pine-emblem
  backings. These add 108 triangles in existing materials, with separate source
  component/contact/camera provenance; no original primitive is replaced or moved
- The original picnic's cloth folds and central tabletop plank contain two tiny
  exposed coincident-face strips, approximately 1.14 square centimetres combined.
  Two separately modeled coral contact-edge bindings, each 8 x 68 x 8 mm,
  cover those strips and meet both original shapes. Their 24 triangles are
  integration-only visible trim; all original coral/cedar buffers remain exact

Full detail is retained for this single close-to-mid-distance lookout pair:
6,152 triangles across 15 source material primitives. The integration adds no
new material batches and makes no claim of measured FPS or GPU improvement.
That original-prop count excludes the scene's new pads and 132 mounting/trim
triangles (nine station mounts plus two picnic bindings).
The selected authoring script reproduces the original props; the game scene
builder independently reproduces the pads, mounting additions and edge bindings.
LOD switching and other Alpine props are outside this selected integration.

See models/alpine-campsite/README.md for reproduction and material mapping.
