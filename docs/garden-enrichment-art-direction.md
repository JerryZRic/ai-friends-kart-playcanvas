# Verdant Canopy: contour gardens and an open nursery

This is incremental, sparse botanical scenery enrichment. The existing
Garden010 is enriched in place. No new map, drivable terrain,
route, vehicle, checkpoint, camera behavior, material, shader, or texture is
introduced. Original hand-authored project geometry is licensed under the
project's existing AGPL-3.0-only terms. This work does not copy proprietary
kart-game assets or architecture.

## Preserved identity

The folded-leaf fan, seven-petal court, seed library, curled fern and crown
bloom remain the five botanical heroes. The open timber arbor remains the
same original architecture. All 21 existing material batches retain their
original indexed vertex, UV, normal and material prefixes. The original
continuous graded terrain remains intact under every addition.

## Composition and placement

Three successive limestone planting beds follow the western ascent. Their
unequal lengths, staggered levels and grouped broad leaves establish near,
middle and far forms while leaving the bend visible. Upper beds follow local
terrain contours to avoid unnecessarily deep front walls.

Two unequal sloping timber roof wings make an open nursery court at the upper
bend. Eight individual whole-footprint column foundations adapt to the
existing hill. There is no oversized raised floor slab. Literal air between
columns, real roof thickness and complete crossbeam bearing surfaces preserve
an open silhouette. A lower planted bed and a smaller far bed connect the
court to the garden rather than filling it with small repeated props.

The return uses two broad, staggered planted beds on the verge opposite the
crown bloom. This is a deliberate change from the initial placement proposal:
the crown-side proposal was outside the unchanged forward camera and occupied
the protected hero reservation farther along the bend. The camera was not
re-aimed to rescue that proposal. The original crown remains unchanged.

Fourteen broad low leaf shells add a lower foliage layer under the taller
groups. One front western bed receives a single warm ivory/coral flower with
an amber heart, tilted toward the natural driving approach. Its supported
stem and real thick petals provide a focal color accent without repeating
flower decorations across the course.

All plants use original thick folded leaf shells, continuous rooted crowns,
soil and retaining rims. Original emerald, lime, teal underside, limestone
and timber materials are reused without tint changes.

## Budget decision

The independently frozen original is 108,557 triangles, 117,223 vertices and
21 batches. The original historical 110,000 ceiling left only 1,442 additive
triangles and was rejected as inadequate for meaningful composed planting.
The approved Garden-only enrichment ceiling is strictly below 120,000 total
triangles, at most 10,000 additive triangles, exactly 21 batches and the
unchanged 200,000 vertex maximum. No global map budget is raised. The immutable
original budget/source oracle remains separate from current-total assertions.

## Honest visual evidence

All comparison cameras were frozen before editing the scene: exact physical
road eye at 1.6 metres, forward target 32 metres ahead, and both unchanged
chase modes with the original safe-camera result. Native projection is 56°
vertical FOV, 1280×800. The five historical catalog cameras are retained
separately; the upward arbor portrait is architectural evidence, not a normal
forward-driving view.

The offline renderer reads exact source indexed meshes and float32 POINT
normal attributes. Its lighting and material response are approximate. These
images do not claim native gameplay, a kart/driver assembly, GPU performance,
frame rate, or universal continuous safety.
