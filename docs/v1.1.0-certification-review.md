that a test run passed. The tagged release's required `release-stable.yml`
workflow and `RELEASE-MANIFEST.json` are the authoritative outcome and exact
source/artefact identities.

## Accepted behavior and honest comparisons

The release retains the accepted development controls, character/build tuning,
six-character cover, seventeen-track race music pool, two fixed item slots and
same-kind stacking. Stacking defaults on at stable, development and desktop
entries; `itemStacking=0` remains an explicit baseline opt-out. No shortcut speed
bonus or new balance tuning is introduced by certification repair.

The old route timings and NPC observations describe their original compiler,
character profiles and complete dependency graph. Those observations remain
separately executable against Git-verified historical source. New current-source
checks cover per-character/build/rate routes, seeded NPC completion, physical
safety and deterministic replay. Some current builds take longer on a narrow
branch. These results are recorded rather than asserting universal shortcut
benefit or claiming that this establishes competitive fairness.

Native lifecycle tests exercise the current queued item-use contract, both fixed
slots, shared cooldown and explicit baseline/default-on behavior. They do not
require an item request to have taken effect before the simulation consumes it.

## Scene and preview scope

Historical prefix, material and assembly recipes retain their exact original
scope and evidence. They do not certify every later decorative append. Current
polish checks retain their actual finite-buffer, vertex-clearance, component and
road-sample assertions; stronger contact/surface checks are reported only for
the component groups they really exercise. Existing live geometry and camera
checks remain enabled. Narrow repairs to demonstrated cosmetic joint overlaps
do not change track layout, terrain, character tuning, item values or palettes.

The concrete scene corrections are bounded: Mountain adds 24 verified cosmetic
contact records, narrows 60 louvers by 3 cm in total and increases six mullion
depths by 2 cm. Town fits 92 door jambs below lintels and 56 crate ends between
slats, removing 678 detected exposed duplicate pairs. Quarry adjusts 36 cosmetic
bin components and matching camera boxes, removing 72 exposed duplicate pairs.
Materials, indices and protected world/road records are unchanged in each
repair; Mountain camera bounds follow only the corrected cosmetic surfaces.
Current Mountain clearance selection now includes all 148 reviewed appended
members while retaining the old complement proof.

The previously blocked Snow checks pass with the reviewed material projection:
full rendered/camera face correspondence and 176 local broadphase queries
(maximum 1,178 candidates from 79,986 camera faces), plus the original terrain
and non-macro contour proof. This does not assert a new whole-assembly contact
certification for decorative roof or platform groups.

Map cards retain their exact historical PNGs and render-source provenance.
Current road geometry, relevant landmark anchors and camera framing are checked
separately. These cards are historical route previews: they may omit newer
cosmetic details and are not represented as newly rendered screenshots.

## Publication gates and limits

The certification branch runs the full suite, production/distribution/source
checks, package validation and native Windows startup smoke. Final main can
reuse that full suite only after the entire exact candidate workflow succeeds
and the final two-parent merge has the identical complete Git tree. The final
main SHA still rebuilds and checks HTML, corresponding source, runtime and
Windows packages, and repeats native Windows startup smoke. The evidence records
both SHAs, tree identity and the successful candidate run/attempt.

Native Windows startup proves the packaged window and renderer can start and
exit in CI. It does not certify GPU gameplay, pointer lock, frame rate or physical
phone behavior. Full HTML retains offline diagnostics and corresponding source;
root Pages and the bounded server runtime link diagnostic users to the exact
v1.1.0 HTML download and source asset. They do not redirect diagnostics to a
floating development build. Existing model, artwork, third-party and separately
licensed music terms remain in effect.
